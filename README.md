# Hi, I'm Muhammad Nashrul Malik 👋

Product designer and electrical engineer moving toward **AI research**, based in Yogyakarta, Indonesia.

I spent the last seven years designing complex enterprise software, including an Arabic-first, right-to-left platform with 13 modules and 1,500+ screens and a design system shared across around 10 products. Before that, my undergraduate research at ITB focused on fault-tolerant systems for autonomous flight. Now I'm bringing that systems thinking to large language models.

## 🔬 Research interests

- **Safety and security of agentic AI:** how harmful instructions reach agents through tools, documents and skills, beyond the user prompt
- **Efficient LLM training and adaptation:** LoRA/PEFT, model merging and mixture-of-experts
- **LLM evaluation:** benchmarks, LLM-as-judge, and cost-aware model selection
- **Multilingual and Southeast Asian NLP:** making models work as well in Indonesian (and Arabic) as in English
- **Human–AI interaction:** designing AI systems people can understand and trust
